# Expert subscription policy — implementation draft, 8 November 2026

This is an owner-authorized default for implementation and review, not a claim of legal approval or live functionality. Keep public checkout in preview until its complete workflow is deployed and verified. Merchant details supplied by the owner are in `src/lib/merchant.ts`.

- Clients remain free. Expert packages cost Start 9.99 EUR, Grow 29.99 EUR and Spotlight 99.99 EUR per calendar month. These are the owner-confirmed final checkout totals; do not add an undisclosed fee or invent a VAT registration number or tax treatment.
- Before the first payment, show the selected package, final EUR amount, service interval, next renewal, cancellation/refund policy version and an unchecked explicit recurring-payment consent. Card entry and storage are handled by the bank. Without a verified bank-stored credential, do not schedule unattended renewal or claim one is active.
- Start the paid interval only after authenticated bank verification of capture. Renew at the original monthly anniversary, clamping short months to their last day without losing the original anchor. Show the timezone. Do not charge in advance or catch up multiple missed months automatically. Changes in price or plan require a fresh, explicit customer choice; no automatic upgrades or prorated surprises.
- Cancellation stops future renewals and preserves access until the verified paid interval ends. Do not renew after deletion is scheduled. An in-flight charge must be reconciled and, if contrary to a preceding cancellation, handled as a refund case; never silently report cancellation as complete while an unresolved debit can still occur.
- On a declined renewal, do not extend paid access and do not automatically retry the card. Notify the customer and offer a bank-hosted, customer-initiated recovery after the outstanding order is reconciled. An ambiguous timeout must be looked up by its existing reference, never charged again as a new order.
- Offer a full refund when requested within 14 days of the first subscription payment, without requiring the customer to waive statutory rights. Review duplicate, unauthorized or otherwise erroneous charges independently of this window. After the initial window, cancellation normally stops future renewals without a routine prorated refund; this does not limit mandatory consumer remedies. Refunds go to the same card through the bank. Confirm completion from the bank before marking a refund complete. Full refund ends the corresponding purchased access; partial refunds require an explicit support decision. Preserve independent admin grants and complimentary access.
- Provide a clear online withdrawal/refund request and support contact, a customer-visible request receipt, payment/cancellation history and durable subscription notices. A request is not a completed financial refund. Implement the workflow before advertising it.
- The owner says DSK issues invoices. This has not been independently established: portal “Invoices” can refer to payment requests. Do not label a gateway transaction confirmation as a tax invoice without verifying the document issuer, contents and accounting requirements. Confirm the applicable sales-document process before live launch.

Primary references: [DSK API](https://uat.dskbank.bg/sandbox/en/integration/api/rest.html), [EU distance-selling disclosures and withdrawal](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm), [NRA sales documents](https://nra.bg/wps/wcm/connect/agency/site/fiskalni-ustroystva-supto-i-e-magazini/dokumenti-za-prodajba.storno.operacii/dokuenti.prodajba/dokumenti.prodajba). Apply the appropriate business/consumer treatment after accounting/legal review; this draft does not assert a tax status.


8 November implementation update: these defaults are reflected in the conditional subscription section of `/terms` and explicit checkout consent. The ledger implements monthly boundaries, no repeated debit on timeout, cancellation, refund-request recording and bank-confirmed revocation. Actual financial refunds are executed by the authorized merchant operator and reconciled by the application. Legal/accounting review and real recurring/customer-email evidence remain activation gates. This document does not certify those gates.
